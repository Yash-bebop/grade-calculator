Evaluation Structure
Mid-sem HALVED (most common):
Internal /20 + MidSem/30 → capped at 15 + EndSem /50 or /70 → scaled to /100
Mid-sem at full value:
Internal(20) + MidSem(30) + EndSem(50) = 100 — only when mid-sem is NOT halved
Mid-sem not held (teacher awards):
Teacher directly awards up to 30 marks. Total raw = Internal(20) + Teacher(30) + EndSem(70) = 120 → scaled to 100

SGPA = Grade points × Credits ÷ Total credits (this semester)
CGPA = Cumulative points ÷ Cumulative credits
Equivalent % = CGPA × 10
Promotion Rules
• Odd → Even semester: promoted regardless of CGPA.
• Even semester (2nd/4th): must achieve CGPA ≥ 4.00 to continue.
• Below 4.00 after even semester → must repeat the academic year.
• No make-up exams. No grace marks.
• Grade improvement: only for C or D, after program completion, within 1 year.
